Candy Wars

Thirty days, one backpack, and a town full of sweets. Buy candy where it’s cheap, sell it where it’s dear, and build the best candy empire in town before the month runs out — a trading game made for kids, playable entirely offline.

  • Seven hangouts, each with its own prices
  • Build a Candy Crew — Promoters, Runners & Hagglers
  • Win stands from rival crews in sell-off mini-games
  • Candy Coins unlock new sweets, stands & games for good
  • No purchases, no chat, no sign-in — ever
  • Plays completely offline
