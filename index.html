Coin & Capsule: Year 2 Maths

Coming soon to Google Play · Android · Ages 6–8

Year 2 maths, turned into a space adventure. Children answer maths questions, earn coins for their effort, and spend them on capsule machines full of cute prizes to collect. Every question is read aloud, every answer shows how it works, and a wrong answer brings a hint — never a dead end.

  • The whole Australian Curriculum Year 2 maths year, across 31 units, plus 8 side units
  • Learn with Australian coins and notes, clocks, shapes and graphs
  • 10 capsule machines and 80 original prizes — no duplicates, ever
  • Coins are earned by playing, never bought
  • Free to try: the first 12 units and every prize. One purchase unlocks the whole year, with no subscription
  • A grown-ups’ area showing progress and common mistakes
  • No ads, no accounts, no data collected

Candy Wars

Coming soon to Google Play · Android · Made for kids

Thirty days, one backpack, and a town full of sweets. Buy candy where it’s cheap, sell it where it’s dear, and build the best candy empire in town before the month runs out — a trading game made for kids, playable entirely offline.

  • Seven hangouts, each with its own prices
  • Build a Candy Crew — Promoters, Runners & Hagglers
  • Win stands from rival crews in sell-off mini-games
  • Candy Coins unlock new sweets, stands & games for good
  • No purchases, no chat, no sign-in — ever
  • Plays completely offline

Tiến Lên: Grand Master

Out now on Google Play · Android

Climb from backyard games to the Grand World Championship in this offline career card game based on Tiến lên (“Thirteen”). Master combos and bombs, conquer every region, and battle friends in local Versus multiplayer.

  • Offline career across six continents
  • Singles, pairs, runs & bombs — the full Tiến Lên rules

For parents

Made for kids, and the grown-ups who look after them

Coin & Capsule and Candy Wars are made for children and follow Google Play’s Families policy. Here is what that means in practice.

No sign-in, no accounts

Nothing to create, nothing to log into. Progress is saved on the device.

No chat with strangers

No messaging, no user content, and no contact between players.

No surprise spending

Coins in our kids’ games are earned by playing, never bought. Any purchase sits behind a grown-up question.

Plain-English privacy

Each game has its own short policy, written so a parent can read it in a few minutes.
