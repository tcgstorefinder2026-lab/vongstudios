Privacy Policy

Coin & Capsule · Effective date: 15 November 2026

App: Coin & Capsule: Year 2 Maths (Android package au.com.year2maths.year2_maths_game)
Developer: Vong Studios ("we", "us")
Contact: vongstudios2026@gmail.com

For parents, in plain terms: Coin & Capsule is a maths game made for children. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no advertisements and no analytics. Everything your child does in the game is saved on the device, and we never see any of it. The app does not connect to the internet itself. The only thing it downloads is its own voice-over, which Google Play delivers after installation.

1. Who this app is for

Coin & Capsule is a child-directed app for children in their second year of school (around ages 6 to 8), distributed under the Google Play Families programme. We treat every user as a child, whatever the device or country. We do not ask for anyone's age.

2. Information the app collects

None. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Progress, answers, coins, prizes, settings and the “For grown-ups” progress report are stored locally on the device only. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else, and deleting the app removes them.

We do not collect or ask for a name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files, microphone or camera input, or any other personal information — from a child or from anyone else. The app does not use the Android advertising ID.

3. Services provided by Google Play

The app uses no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or tracking. Two things are handled by Google Play itself, under the Google Privacy Policy:

  • Voice-over download. The game's spoken instructions are a separate download that Google Play delivers after the app is installed, in the same way it delivers the app. Nothing about your child is sent to us as part of this.
  • The full-version purchase. The first part of the game is free. The rest can be unlocked with a single one-off purchase made through Google Play. The purchase screen sits behind a grown-up question that a young child cannot answer. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see card or account details. We receive only Google's confirmation that the purchase was made.

4. Coins and capsule prizes

Coins in the game are earned only by answering maths questions. They can never be bought with real money and have no value outside the game. Capsule prizes are won with those earned coins, and every machine shows its full line-up before a coin is spent.

5. Device backup

If the device's own backup is switched on (for example Android's backup to the family's Google account), Android may include the game's saved progress in that backup, so it returns when the app is reinstalled. That backup belongs to the device owner and is managed by Android and Google. We have no access to it.

6. What the app deliberately does not do

  • No advertisements of any kind.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, attribution or tracking.
  • No accounts, logins, cloud saves or leaderboards.
  • No chat, messaging, user-generated content or contact between players.
  • No links out to websites or social networks, and no sharing.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or file access.
  • No coins, lives or advantages sold for real money.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information. We hold none.

7. Children’s privacy (COPPA, GDPR-K and equivalents)

Because Coin & Capsule is made for children, we comply with the Australian Privacy Act 1988, the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States, the child-protection provisions of the UK and EU GDPR, and equivalent laws elsewhere.

We do not collect personal information from children, or from anyone. There is nothing for a parent to review, correct or delete on our side, and no parental consent is needed for the app to work.

8. Data retention

We retain no personal data, because we collect none. Game data stays on the device until the app's data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. We hold no personal data, so there is nothing for us to provide. Questions about Google Play purchases or backups should go to Google. You are always welcome to contact us and we will help where we can.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes what it does with information, this policy will be updated first, the effective date above will change, and the current version will always be at this address.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy, this app, or your child’s data: vongstudios2026@gmail.com
